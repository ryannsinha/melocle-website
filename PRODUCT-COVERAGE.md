# Product story and coverage

The website follows a buyer journey: attention → familiarity → relevant interest → contextual outreach → conversation follow-through.

Content Studio, Social Chorus, signals and outreach remain central. Unibox completes the handoff into replies and next steps. Outreach prioritizes leads discovered through signals and post engagement; importing an existing list is an additional option.

## Coverage retained

- Content creation, research, review, calendar and publishing for LinkedIn and X.
- Social Chorus: shared briefs, individual perspectives, voluntary employee participation and reviews.
- Brand Brain and personal Second Brain foundations.
- All 36 signal types, represented by coverage rather than agent characters: buying intent (10), engagement (10), market/content (9), career moves (6) and funding (1). Additional-source requirements remain labelled. The copy distinguishes buying intent from other research and context signals.
- Lead discovery, research, enrichment, qualification, lists and optional CSV/XLSX import.
- Outreach preparation, approvals, sequence steps and Unibox follow-up.
- Lead magnets, warm introductions and partner relationship workflows, presented in a larger two-card section with workflow previews and early-access entry points.
- Dashboard 2’s Mello design, with examples spanning content, Social Chorus, signal interpretation and outreach preparation.
- Five persona use cases, each with one goal and three workflow steps: founders, marketing teams, sales/RevOps, experts/consultants and agencies.
- Eight interactive workspace views immediately beneath the hero. The opening view connects one draft, team-sharing story, engagement signal, outreach message and reply. The outreach view displays the source post, relevant response and customer-fit review beside the message. Mobile views are concise, with an expansion option.

The full 33-capability research remains in `reference/platform-capabilities.js` for reference; it is not displayed as a long marketing catalog. Detail dialogs keep full signal coverage and brand knowledge accessible without crowding the five main product cards.

## Current presentation

The hero stays white and text-led, with restrained workflow icons and checkbox texture. The blurred navigation adapts to the chapter beneath it. Dark chapters, a black product submenu, compact stacked persona stories and the blue closing action retain the premium visual rhythm. Both stacks use native scrolling and readable fallbacks for reduced motion, short viewports and enlarged text spacing.

No repeated feature catalog, extra journey summary or duplicate workspace tour has been added. The sticky use-case heading stays above its menu, and the right-hand stories retain the previously approved three-step copy.

## Early access and evidence

The offer is request-based access, with availability, setup, any pricing and next steps confirmed individually. No response time, free pilot or guaranteed availability is promised.

The request flow saves to a local SQLite database and preserves the precise persona, optional starting workflow, first/latest acquisition and CTA context. The local review desk supports ownership, notes and manual stages. No email, invitation, external submission or product account is created. The demonstration workspace is not customer outcome evidence. The marketing copy uses confident capability and benefit claims; no customer testimonials or quantitative results are manufactured.

Product availability and source requirements still need verification before public launch. The local readiness desk leaves these gates unverified until evidence is recorded. No Dashboard 2 frontend edits or deployments were made.
