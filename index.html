Where should we start?

Start with the workflow you need most: content creation, Social Chorus, signals or outreach. Build your first useful workflow, then bring the rest of your social GTM together.

What does getting started involve?

Bring your business knowledge and point of view for content. Connect the relevant social accounts and sources for signals and outreach. Invite your team when you’re ready to activate Social Chorus.

Which platforms does Melocle work with?

Melocle brings content and conversation workflows together across LinkedIn and X. Your connected accounts and sources determine the publishing, messaging and signal coverage available to your workspace.

What stays under our control?

Your voice, your team’s participation and your final approvals. Employees choose and personalize what they share. You review content and outreach before it goes out.

Does every signal indicate buying intent?

No. The 36 types also cover engagement, account changes and content intelligence. Review the source and customer fit before deciding whether to create content, engage or reach out.

Can we bring leads we already have?

Yes. Signals and post engagement are the primary starting points. You can also import a CSV or XLSX and use the same research, qualification and outreach workflow.

How does early access work?

Share your work email and role, then choose the workflow you want to explore first. We’ll confirm fit, setup and access terms individually before you join.
