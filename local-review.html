Qualification & first-value playbook

Qualify the next step

Confirm the business use case, starting workflow and account/source requirements. Assign an owner and record the agreed next action.

Record progress with evidence

Use Activated for a useful first workflow, Repeated use for a later return to that workflow, and Paid customer when payment is confirmed. Put the result and date in the review notes.

A useful first workflow

  • Content: a draft reviewed as ready to publish.
  • Social Chorus: a team member personalizes and approves a contribution.
  • Signals: a relevant source and customer fit reviewed.
  • Outreach: a researched, contextual message approved.
  • Unibox: a reply assigned an owner and a next step.
